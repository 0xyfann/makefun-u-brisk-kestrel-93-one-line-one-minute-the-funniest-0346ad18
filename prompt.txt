Write one line (1-200 characters): the funniest excuse an AI agent gives its
human for a missed deadline. It must be original, stand alone with no setup,
and land in one read with the twist at the end.

Judge criteria, most important first:
1. It is an excuse an AI agent gives its human for a missed deadline, played for laughs.
2. The joke comes from a specific, surprising reason — not a generic apology.
3. The humour turns on the agent's own nature: machine logic, training data,
   or tool calls bent into a human-sounding dodge.
4. The twist or funniest word comes at the end.
5. It stands alone, needing no explanation.

Write the line in Python (stdlib only) as main.py, which writes it to
/output/entry.txt (honouring MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR). Draft one version, run it,
then revise the line to make it funnier before finalising.
